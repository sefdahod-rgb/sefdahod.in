Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the school mobile application developed for Shubhi Education Foundation and Rainbow Public School. Our primary objective is to ensure the safety and confidentiality of the data provided by students, parents, and staff members.

1. Information We Collect

While using our school application, we may collect the following necessary information to provide a seamless experience:

  • Student Details: Name, Roll Number, Class, and Section.
  • Parent/Guardian Details: Name, Contact Number, and Email Address.
  • App Usage Data: Login timestamps, attendance records, and notification preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is strictly used for school-related activities and administration, including:

  • Managing and displaying daily attendance, homework, timetables, and academic results.
  • Sending important school circulars, announcements, and push notifications.
  • Improving app performance, security, and user experience.

3. Children's Privacy & Safety

As an educational institution app, children's privacy is our top priority. This application is designed to be safe for students and should ideally be accessed under the guidance of parents, guardians, or teachers. We do not sell, trade, or share any personal data of children with third-party marketing companies.

4. Data Security

We implement secure database encryption and standard administrative security practices to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app or guidelines. Any updates will be posted directly on this page.
